First circuit

  1. Start Spock, then press Enter.
  2. Click the file button at the far bottom right.
  3. Choose Ler Circuito (load), click the Nome field, enter AMP, press Enter, then click LER.
  4. Click Plot near the bottom left to plot.

To keep edits, use Gravar Circuito in the file menu, enter a name, then click GRAVAR. Browser storage copies DOS files, not unsaved edits or the running screen.

Saved DOS files are copied to browser storage every 5 seconds. Use “Save to browser” and wait for it to finish before closing. Download important work.
